A practical comparison framework

Read beyond the product thumbnail.

Keep the same fields for every candidate. Consistent notes make differences in fit, specification and cost easier to see.

Suggested fields for your personal shopping spreadsheet
Field What to record Why it helps
Item and variant Product link, selected color, size and model. Prevents comparisons between different versions.
Measurements Relevant size-chart measurements and units. Gives you a clearer fit reference than the label alone.
Materials Stated composition, lining, hardware and finish. Helps assess intended use and care requirements.
Evidence Recent listing photos, seller details and dated notes. Makes claims easier to revisit and verify.
Estimated cost Item price, delivery, service charges and applicable taxes. Supports a comparison based on the full budget.
Last checked Date of your latest availability and price review. Flags information that may have become outdated.
Litbuy shopping guide

Build a shortlist you can explain.

Begin with a specific need: everyday sneakers, a warmer layer, an adjustable cap or a finishing accessory. Browse the matching category and save only the candidates that fit that purpose.

Next, compare each item against something you already know. A well-fitting sweatshirt provides a useful measurement reference. A pair of comfortable shoes helps you identify the fit details you need to check.

Finally, review the current listing before making a decision. Spreadsheet notes can become outdated, and a familiar brand name or attractive image does not establish authenticity.

Before you decide

Six checks for a stronger shortlist

  • Confirm that the link matches the exact item and selected variant.
  • Compare measurements and check whether units are centimeters or inches.
  • Read the stated materials and relevant care information.
  • Check recent photos and the source of any quality claims.
  • Include delivery, fees and applicable taxes in your budget.
  • Review availability and return terms before purchasing.

Litbuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping reference organized around product links and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy and freshness of each entry; a listing alone does not verify quality, authenticity or availability.

How should I compare spreadsheet finds?

Compare the exact item, selected variant, measurements, materials, seller information, recent photos and estimated total cost. Record the date of each check so older notes are easy to identify.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping and taxes?

Do not assume it does. A listed amount may cover only the item. Confirm domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes before deciding.

How do I choose clothing and shoe sizes?

Use the listing’s measurements and size chart. Compare garment measurements with clothing that fits you, and compare shoe sizing with foot length and the relevant brand chart. Size labels can vary.
